Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, go to checkout, enter your delivery address and pay by UPI, card, net banking or cash on delivery.

What are the delivery charges?

Delivery is free on orders above ₹499 after discounts. Below that, a small charge based on your PIN code is shown at checkout.

How long will delivery take?

Enter your PIN code on any product page or at checkout to see the estimated delivery time.

Is cash on delivery available?

COD is available on most PIN codes for orders up to ₹5,000. Checkout shows whether COD is available for your address.

Can I cancel my order?

Yes, from My Account until the order is shipped. Prepaid orders are refunded to the original payment method.

How do returns work?

Delivered orders can be returned from My Account under our Return Policy.

Where can I find ingredients and shelf life?

They are printed on every pack and will be listed on each product page once confirmed.

Do you supply in bulk?

Yes. Bakeries, sweet shops, hotels, retailers and distributors can send an enquiry from the B2B page or on WhatsApp.

How do I eat gulkand?

Most people eat a spoonful on its own after meals, stir it into cold milk, or use it on paan, ice cream and desserts.

Is karonda cherry the same as a real cherry?

Karonda cherry is made from karonda berries, candied and coloured. It is the cherry commonly used in Indian bakeries for cakes and desserts.

How do I stop tutti frutti from sinking in a cake?

Toss the tutti frutti in a spoonful of flour before folding it into the batter. This helps it stay spread through the cake.

What is the difference between tutti frutti and lacha tutti frutti?

Tutti frutti is cut into small cubes and is best inside cakes and cookies. Lacha tutti frutti is cut into thin strips and is best as a garnish on top.

Which pack sizes are available?

100 g, 200 g and 500 g packs. Bulk packs are available for retailers and caterers.
