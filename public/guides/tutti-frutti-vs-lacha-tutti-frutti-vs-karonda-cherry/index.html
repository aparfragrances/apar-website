Tutti frutti, lacha tutti frutti or karonda cherry: which should you use?

All three are candied, colourful and sweet, but each works best in a different place. Here is a quick guide.

Tutti frutti: best inside bakes

Small cubes that hold their shape in the oven. Use inside cakes, fruit bread, buns and cookies, and in ice cream and kulfi.

Lacha tutti frutti: best as a garnish

Thin strips that spread evenly on top. Use on barfi, halwa, kheer, ice cream, falooda and meetha paan.

Karonda cherry: best for decoration

Whole glossy red cherries with a sweet-tangy taste. Use on top of cream cakes and cupcakes, or chopped into fruit cake.
