Easy eggless tutti frutti cake

This soft tea-time cake needs no eggs and uses everyday ingredients. The colourful pieces of tutti frutti make every slice look festive.

Ingredients

  • 1½ cups (190 g) maida (all-purpose flour)
  • ¾ cup (150 g) powdered sugar
  • 1 cup (240 g) curd, at room temperature
  • ½ cup (120 ml) oil or melted butter
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • ½ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp vanilla essence
  • A pinch of salt
  • ½ cup APAR Tutti Frutti + 1 tbsp flour to coat

Method

  1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C and line a loaf tin.
  2. Whisk curd and sugar until smooth. Add baking powder and baking soda and let it bubble for 2 minutes.
  3. Whisk in oil and vanilla.
  4. Sift in flour and salt and fold gently until just combined.
  5. Toss the tutti frutti in 1 tbsp flour and fold most of it in. Pour into the tin and scatter the rest on top.
  6. Bake for 35–40 minutes until a skewer comes out clean. Cool before slicing.

Tips

  • Coating tutti frutti in flour stops it from sinking.
  • Add a few chopped karonda cherries for extra colour.
  • Do not over-mix the batter, or the cake will be dense.
