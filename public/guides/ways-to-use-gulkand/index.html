10 delicious ways to use gulkand

Gulkand, the sweet rose petal preserve, is one of the most versatile treats in an Indian kitchen. Here are ten simple ways to enjoy a jar of APAR Gulkand.

1. Gulkand milk

Stir one or two teaspoons of gulkand into a glass of chilled milk. It is a classic summer drink in many Indian homes.

2. Gulkand shake

Blend cold milk, a scoop of vanilla ice cream and a tablespoon of gulkand. Top with chopped nuts.

3. Meetha paan

Gulkand is the heart of a good meetha paan, along with lacha tutti frutti, coconut and fennel.

4. Ice cream topping

Spoon gulkand over vanilla or kesar-pista ice cream for an instant rose sundae.

5. Gulkand lassi

Whisk thick curd, a little sugar and gulkand with ice for a fragrant rose lassi.

6. Laddoo and barfi filling

Use gulkand as a filling for coconut laddoo, besan laddoo or barfi.

7. Gulkand kulfi

Fold gulkand into thickened milk before freezing kulfi for a beautiful rose swirl.

8. Falooda

Layer gulkand with falooda sev, basil seeds, milk and ice cream.

9. With curd or yoghurt

Swirl a spoonful into plain yoghurt as a quick dessert.

10. On its own

A small spoonful after a meal is the simplest way to enjoy it.

Keep the jar tightly closed and always use a clean, dry spoon.
