What is gulkand?

The word gulkand comes from "gul" (rose) and "qand" (sweet). Rose petals are layered with sugar and allowed to mature, giving a deep rose flavour and a soft, spreadable texture. In many Indian homes it is traditionally enjoyed during hot summer months as a cooling treat.

How to use gulkand

  • Stir 1–2 teaspoons into cold milk for gulkand milk
  • Blend with milk and ice cream for a gulkand shake
  • Classic filling for meetha paan
  • Topping for vanilla ice cream, kulfi and falooda
  • Filling for laddoo, barfi, modak and sandesh
  • Mix into thick curd or lassi
  • Spread on toast or serve with plain yoghurt
