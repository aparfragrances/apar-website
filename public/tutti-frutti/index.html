What is tutti frutti?

Tutti frutti means "all fruits" in Italian. In India it is traditionally made from fruit (often raw papaya) that is cut into small cubes, candied in sugar syrup and coloured red, green and yellow. It holds its shape in baking, which is why it is so popular in cakes and breads.

Questions about this product

How do I stop tutti frutti from sinking in a cake?

Toss the tutti frutti in a spoonful of flour before folding it into the batter. This helps it stay spread through the cake.
