About APAR

Traditional Indian flavours from Jaipur, since 2017

APAR is the consumer brand of Apar Fragrances, a family business in Jaipur, Rajasthan, that has been making traditional Indian sweet ingredients and mouth fresheners since 2017.

APAR is being developed as a premium Indian food products brand focused on traditional Indian flavours and contemporary premium presentation.

The initial product range includes Gulkand, Karonda Cherry, Tutti Frutti, Lacha Tutti Frutti, Lacha Supari and curated combo/gift products.

The brand aims to combine traditional Indian taste with modern quality standards, premium presentation, attractive packaging and a convenient online shopping experience.

Our products

  • APAR Gulkand: Rose petal preserve, made in Jaipur
  • APAR Karonda Cherry: Sweet and tangy candied cherries for bakes and desserts
  • APAR Tutti Frutti: Coloured candied fruit, crafted for modern kitchens
  • APAR Lacha Tutti Frutti: Premium candied strips with a distinctive texture
  • APAR Lacha Supari: Premium mouth freshener, presented with care
  • Gift boxes and combos
