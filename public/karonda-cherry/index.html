What is karonda cherry?

Karonda (Carissa carandas) is a small, sour Indian berry. When it is candied in sugar syrup and coloured, it becomes the glossy red "cherry" that Indian bakeries have used for generations on cakes, pastries and puddings.

How to use karonda cherry

  • Topping for cream cakes and cupcakes
  • Chopped into fruit cake, plum cake and cookies
  • Garnish for ice cream sundaes and falooda
  • Decoration for custard, trifle and puddings
  • Colour and bite in kulfi and shrikhand
