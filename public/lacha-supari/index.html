What is lacha supari?

Supari is areca nut. Lacha supari is supari cut into thin, delicate shreds and flavoured, so it is easy to chew and pleasant to serve as a mukhwas (mouth freshener) after food.

How to use lacha supari

  • After-meal mouth freshener
  • Ingredient in meetha paan
  • Serving guests at home and events
